What Is a Solver? How Our Solver Fixes Everyday File Headaches

OurSolver editorial · 2026-10-30 · 3 min read

Understand what a digital solver is and how Our Solver eliminates image size, dimension, and PDF layout roadblocks on your device.

When people ask “What is a solver?”, they are usually looking for a way out of a stubborn technical roadblock. In computing and digital media, a solver is a dedicated utility or algorithm that takes a set of strict constraints—such as an upload form requiring an exact 600 × 600 pixel square under 200 KB—and calculates a clean, working solution without manual guesswork.

What Is a Solver in Everyday Computing?

Every digital document or picture comes with underlying mathematical constraints: pixel dimensions, color bit-depth, compression quantization, and container encoding. When an online portal, email client, or social network rejects your file, it is because your file violates one or more of those constraints.

A generic converter simply changes a file extension, often leaving the root problem untouched. A true digital solver, by contrast, analyzes the conflict, adjusts the geometry or byte structure, and outputs a compliant file that meets the destination's rules.

Why an In-Browser Solver Is a Better Approach

Traditional online tools force you to upload personal documents, ID cards, and photos to third-party cloud servers. This introduces three major risks: privacy exposure, slow upload/download queues, and aggressive automated degradation.

Our Solver operates entirely on client-side browser technology. When you drop an image or PDF into Our Solver, the transformation executes directly in your computer's local memory using HTML5 Canvas and WebAssembly. Your files are never transmitted across the network, meaning confidential contracts and private photos stay 100% on your device.
