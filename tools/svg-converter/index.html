    Good to know

    Because SVG is a resolution-independent vector format, OurSolver scales the vector paths directly to your target width and height before rasterizing—giving you razor-sharp edges at any size from a 16×16 favicon to a 4,000×4,000 print logo.

    Keep your original files. Check the downloaded result before submitting or sharing it. Modern desktop browsers work best for larger documents.

    Does OurSolver store my files?

    No. Files are held in browser memory during processing. Clear the files or close the page when you finish. Downloaded files remain on your device.

    Why did processing fail?

    A file may be damaged, encrypted, unsupported or too large for your device's available memory. Try a smaller file and an updated browser. Read troubleshooting tips.
